A web app for planning *Skyrim* potions with kryptopyr's *Complete Alchemy and Cooking Overhaul* (CACO) installed. It also covers the ingredients added by *Beyond Skyrim: Bruma* and *Hunterborn*. Served as a static site from the repo root with a live GitHub Pages deploy.

- Search ingredients by name, or filter by a combination of effects.
- Add up to three ingredients to a potion and preview each effect's relative magnitude.
- Include or hide the Bruma and Hunterborn ingredients. Ingredient lookup handles object-form effects.
- **CACO Crafting Guide:** an interactive cheat sheet for bombs, waxes, distillation, extracts, salves, mortars, the crucible, cooking, blood alchemy and the Alchemy perk tree. It includes a perk planner that dims recipes your character can't craft yet. Redesigned with a segmented-control nav between pages.
- The broader CACO + CCOR guide now lives in [caco-ccor-guide](https://github.com/Shanki200801/caco-ccor-guide).
